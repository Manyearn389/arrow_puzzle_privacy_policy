Privacy Policy for Arrow Puzzle

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 Last Updated: September 5, 2026 App Name: Arrow Puzzle

This Privacy Policy describes how Arrow Puzzle ("we", "us", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you download, install, access, or play our mobile game.

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This policy is written in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Important Summary: Arrow Puzzle does NOT require account registration, personal profile creation, login credentials, or direct personal identification (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number) to play. Game state and settings are saved locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We categorize the information handled by or through the Application into two main categories:

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress & Stats: Solved levels, star ratings, high scores, coins, time records, and achievements.
  • User Preferences: Audio toggle states (sound effects, music), vibration setting, and UI theme selection (Dark/Light).

Note: This data is stored strictly on your local device storage (HTML5 Local Storage / App Sandbox) and is never transmitted to our private servers.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

When you use Arrow Puzzle, third-party ad networks and platform services (such as Google AdSense / Google AdMob and Google Play Services) may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personally identifiable device data, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID, or unique vendor IDs.
  • Device & Network Information: Device make and model, operating system version, carrier, network connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile), locale, time zone, and language settings.
  • Ad Interaction & Analytics Data: Ad views, clicks, banner impressions, video ad completion rates for rewarded ads, and crash logs.
  • Approximate Geolocation: General city- or country-level location derived from IP address (precise GPS location is never requested or collected).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Gameplay Functionality: To save your puzzle progress, coins, unlocked levels, and preferences.
  • Monetization & Free Access: To display banner advertisements and rewarded video advertisements that allow players to earn free hints, bonus coins, and level perks.
  • App Performance & Stability: To detect crashes, diagnose technical bugs, and optimize app performance across various Android devices.
  • Compliance & Security: To prevent fraudulent ad clicks, defend against automated abuse, and comply with Google Play security guidelines.

4. Advertising & Opt-Out Preferences

Our app serves in-game advertisements provided by Google. Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, ads may be personalized (tailored to your interests) or non-personalized (contextual).

How to Opt Out or Reset Your Advertising ID on Android:

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy on newer Android versions).
  3. Select Ads (or Ads Privacy).
  4. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID, or toggle on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

You can also manage your Google Ad settings globally at https://adssettings.google.com.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Arrow Puzzle is designed for general audiences and puzzle lovers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at manyearn389@gmail.com. We will promptly investigate and ensure any such data is deleted from our systems and partner logs.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because your gameplay data is stored strictly on your device:

  • In-App Reset: You can reset all gameplay progress, coins, and high scores at any time via Settings → Reset All Progress inside the game.
  • Uninstall / Clear Data: You can delete all local data by clearing the app's cache and data through Android's Application Settings or by uninstalling the Application.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

For Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom (GDPR):

You are entitled to rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time.

For Users in California (CCPA / CPRA):

California residents have the right to request disclosure of personal information collected, request deletion of personal information, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal data for monetary consideration.

8. Security
